# RAMP Soft Gating Engine Specification

**SIH26080 — Regime-Aware AI Post-Processing of Monsoon Rainfall Forecasts**  
**Ministry of Earth Sciences (MoES) | National Centre for Medium Range Weather Forecasting (NCMRWF)**

---

## 1. Gating Mathematical Formulation

The RAMP soft gating engine consumes the continuous calibrated probability vector generated by the Phase 4 Weather Regime Intelligence Engine:

$$\mathbf{p}(x) = [p_0(x), p_1(x), p_2(x), p_3(x), p_4(x), p_5(x), p_6(x)]^T$$

The final rainfall forecast is the convex combination:

$$\text{RAMP}(x) = \sum_{k=0}^{6} p_k(x) \cdot \text{Expert}_k(x)$$

---

## 2. Invariants & Strict Validation

Gating vectors are instantiated and validated via `GateWeights`:

1. **Non-Negativity Constraint:**
   $$p_k \ge 0.0 \quad \forall k \in \{0, \dots, 6\}$$
2. **Sum-to-One Normalization:**
   $$\left| \sum_{k=0}^{6} p_k - 1.0 \right| \le 10^{-4}$$

### Loud Failure Behavior
If an input probability vector violates either constraint (e.g. sum = 1.2 or $p_k < 0$), the engine raises a `ValueError` immediately:
```
[GATING INVARIANT VIOLATION] Regime gate weights must sum to 1.0 +/- 1e-4. Observed sum = 1.200000 across components.
```
Corrupt or uncalibrated vectors are never silently re-normalized unless explicitly requested by the Phase 4 calibration service contract.

---

## 3. Gating Modes & Ablation Baselines

The gating engine supports four operational and ablation modes:

| Gating Mode | Mathematical Definition | Role |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **`soft_gating`** | $w_k = p_k(x)$ (Phase 4 Calibrated Probabilities) | **Primary RAMP Architecture** |
| **`hard_argmax`** | $w_k = 1.0$ if $k = \arg\max_j p_j(x)$, else $0.0$ | Ablation Baseline B |
| **`uniform_gating`**| $w_k = 1/7$ for all $k \in \{0..6\}$ | Ablation Baseline E |
| **`global_ml_only`**| Bypasses experts entirely; routes to Global ML | Ablation Baseline A |

---

## 4. Uncertainty & Transition Awareness

Phase 4 provides Shannon entropy $H(\mathbf{p})$ and normalized entropy $\tilde{H}(\mathbf{p})$:

$$H(\mathbf{p}) = -\sum_{k=0}^{6} p_k \ln(p_k)$$

When uncertainty-aware gating is optionally enabled:
- **LOW Uncertainty:** $100\%$ pure RAMP soft gating.
- **MEDIUM Uncertainty:** $80\%$ RAMP $+ 20\%$ Global ML.
- **HIGH Uncertainty:** $50\%$ RAMP $+ 50\%$ Global ML.

*Default behavior:* In accordance with Phase 6 requirements, pure calibrated soft gating remains the default operational mode.
